# MouseTip

## 0.3.0

- **Starts on the right input:** after logging in, MouseTip now starts the way you last played on that computer: on a handheld, tooltips go straight to the lower right corner instead of the cursor until you touched the controller. It still switches the moment you change between the controller and the mouse.
- **Tooltip size**, set apart for the mouse and for a controller (bigger text on a handheld, the usual size on a PC): two rows in the options, or `/mousetip size` for the input you're using. At 100% the game's own size is left alone.
- Listed in the game's own Options > AddOns. Key bindings to open the options and to switch MouseTip on or off. The options window remembers where you put it. A one-time line on first login says what MouseTip does; another says so if ElvUI, TipTac or a similar addon also places tooltips. `/mousetip debug` for bug reports.

## 0.2.0

- **With a controller, tooltips go to the lower right corner** of the screen, where there is no mouse pointer to follow. As soon as you use the mouse again they follow the cursor; pick up the controller and they go back to the corner. On by default; turn it off in the options (**With a controller: lower right corner**) or with `/mousetip pad`.

## 0.1.3

- **Controller:** when a fight starts, B goes straight back to its usual job, even with a MouseTip window open (it used to stay on closing the window until the fight ended). It closes the window again once the fight is over.

## 0.1.2

- **Controller:** the B button now closes MouseTip's window, like the X and Escape. While one is open, B closes it; once it is closed, B does whatever you have it set to again.
